📐 Theoretical Foundation: Additive Efficiency Axiom of SHAP ▼

In cooperative game theory, Shapley values uniquely satisfy four fundamental axioms: Efficiency, Symmetry, Dummy Player, and Additivity. Because the categorical variables (Department and Career Path) are transformed via one-hot dummy encoding inside a leak-safe ColumnTransformer, their transformed dimensionality expands (e.g. 155 one-hot columns for Overall, 114 for Daffodil).

By the Additive Property of SHAP, summing the SHAP values across disjoint dummy indicator variables for a mutually exclusive categorical feature yields the exact net marginal contribution of that categorical feature to the model output:

φ(Department) = ∑_{c ∈ Categories} φ(OneHot_{c})

This guarantees zero information loss while projecting attributions faithfully back to the 17 verified research features.

⚖️ Non-Causal Research Disclaimer ▼

SHAP values indicate mathematical feature importance and marginal shift in the classifier's output score for a specific input configuration. They do not establish causal etiology. A positive contribution for "Software Engineer" indicates that within the surveyed sample, students pursuing software roles statistically reported higher career anxiety, not that pursuing software engineering is causally responsible for career anxiety.

📂 1. Dataset & Target Formulation

Source Survey Data: Secondary survey dataset of undergraduate university students in Bangladesh (Career_Anxiety_due_to_AI.xlsx).

Raw Sample: 3,156 completed survey entries.

Inclusion Protocol: Restricting population exclusively to 2nd, 3rd, and 4th-year students actively engaged in major studies. 1st-year students ($N=1,120$) were filtered out to focus on senior undergraduates in active career transition.

Analytical Sample Size: 2,036 students.

Engineered Target Operationalization (Anxiety_Label):

Raw survey column career_anxiety binarized into:

  • Class 0 (32.4%): No Anxiety + Low
  • Class 1 (67.6%): Medium + High (Elevated Career Anxiety)

🛡️ 2. Leak-Safe Preprocessing Protocol

To eliminate data leakage, preprocessing is strictly encapsulated inside sklearn.compose.ColumnTransformer within an end-to-end Pipeline:

Numerical Pipeline:
SimpleImputer(strategy='median') → StandardScaler()
Categorical Pipeline:
SimpleImputer(strategy='most_frequent') → OneHotEncoder(handle_unknown='ignore')

Strict Zero-Leakage: All imputation medians and scaling parameters are fitted exclusively on the training split ($X_{\text{train}}$) and applied downstream to test and runtime inference without refitting.

🧪 The 17 Verified Predictive Features

Extracted Faithfully from Experimental Analysis

Perceived_Urgency replace × takeover

An engineered composite feature multiplying belief in job replacement (0–2) by estimated takeover timeline (0–5). Not a validated latent psychological construct.

Risk_Knowledge_Gap future − knowledge

An engineered numerical difference between AI future-displacement perspective and self-assessed AI knowledge. Not a validated latent construct.

Age_Year_Ratio age ÷ academic_year

An engineered ratio of chronological age to academic year.

Threat_Perception Binary response indicator

Indicates whether the respondent provided a substantive response to the AI-tool perception item. This is not a validated psychological threat-perception scale.

Total_AI_Tools Count(tools)

Number of AI tools reported in the comma-separated survey response.

Tool Subtypes Text / Code / Creative

Three separate binary flags detecting reported use of generative text (ChatGPT/Claude), code generation (Copilot/Cursor), or creative tools (Midjourney/Canva).

⚠️ Methodological Limitations & Ethical Disclaimers

Academic Research Scope & Boundaries

1. Strictly Non-Clinical & Non-Diagnostic Instrument

This system is not a medical, psychiatric, or psychological diagnostic tool. The target variable reflects a single subjective Likert survey response operationalized into empirical groups: Class 0 (No Anxiety / Low) vs Class 1 (Medium / High). It has not been calibrated against clinical psychometric scales (such as GAD-7) and must never be used for psychiatric evaluation or patient triage.

2. Observational & Associative Nature (Non-Causal)

Machine learning models identify statistical correlations and feature co-occurrences in the survey dataset. They do not prove causal relationships. An elevated SHAP attribution does not indicate that AI perceptions cause clinical anxiety.

3. Survey Self-Reporting Biases

Data was collected through self-administered questionnaires. Results are subject to self-selection bias, social desirability bias, and subjective variations in how students quantify their own AI knowledge.

4. Geographic & Cohort Specificity

The analytical cohort ($N=2,036$) represents undergraduate students enrolled in universities in Bangladesh. Generalizing these empirical models to international populations or experienced professionals requires independent validation.

5. Nested Institutional Cohorts

Daffodil International University ($N=695$) is an institutional sub-cohort nested within the Private University cohort ($N=1,168$). Their populations overlap and should not be interpreted as mutually exclusive datasets.
